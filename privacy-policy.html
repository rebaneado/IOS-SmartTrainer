SmartTrainer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-17

SmartTrainer is an app for controlling a Bluetooth smart trainer and recording indoor rides. This page explains what data the app touches and where it goes.

Data storage

All workout data, ride recordings, and settings (FTP, heart-rate zones) are stored only on your device, using Apple's standard on-device storage. None of it is sent to, or visible to, the developer. The app has no user accounts and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Bluetooth

The app connects directly to your smart trainer and, optionally, a heart-rate strap over Bluetooth to read sensor data and control resistance. This connection is local, device-to-device — no Bluetooth data leaves your phone or tablet.

Strava (optional)

If you choose to connect a Strava account, the app uses Strava's standard OAuth sign-in. You authenticate directly with Strava, not with the app developer. The resulting access token is stored only on your device (in the iOS Keychain). When you tap "Log ride to Strava," your ride file is sent directly to Strava's API.

To keep the app from having to embed a secret credential that could be extracted from the app binary, the one-time token exchange during sign-in passes through a small proxy server operated by the developer solely for this purpose. That server does not log, store, or retain any data — it forwards the request to Strava and returns Strava's response. If you never connect Strava, this server is never contacted.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't require an account with us.

Third-party services

If you use the optional Strava integration, your use of Strava is also governed by Strava's own privacy policy.
